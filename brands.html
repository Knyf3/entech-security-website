ACRE
Formerly: Vanderbilt Industries / Siemens Security

ACRE Security

ACRE (Access Control Related Enterprises) was formed when Vanderbilt International acquired key security brands: ACT from Ireland (access control), CEM Systems (access & video), and the former Vanderbilt portfolio (SPC intrusion, detection). The ACRE brand was officially launched in 2023, consolidating all these into one cohesive family.

PT Entech has distributed Vanderbilt/ACT products since the brand's availability in Indonesia, and continues as authorized distributor under the ACRE brand.

SIEMENS
SiPass Product Line

Siemens SiPass

Siemens is one of the world's largest industrial conglomerates, and their SiPass product line represents their IP-based access control system. SiPass Entro and SiPass Integrated offer scalable, enterprise-grade access control with deep integration capabilities for elevators, building management, and HR systems.

PT Entech has been an authorized Siemens Security distributor since founding in 2012, and remains an authorized SiPass distributor. Landmark deployments include Pacific Place Jakarta (200+ doors) and Menara Kompas (48 doors with KONE lift integration).

DETEX
Emergency Exit Hardware

DETEX Corporation

DETEX, founded in 1903, is a leading US manufacturer of exit and emergency access hardware. Their products range from exit push bars and rim exit devices to door prop alarms and perimeter security hardware — all built to life-safety standards.

The V40 Rim Exit Device is DETEX's flagship product — ANSI Grade 1 rated, fire-rated, and capable of withstanding 1,400+ lbs of force — widely specified for commercial and institutional buildings across Indonesia.

NEDAP
Long Range Identification

Nedap Security Management

Nedap is a Dutch technology company specializing in long-range UHF RFID identification for access control applications. Their uPASS and TRANSIT product lines enable hands-free vehicle and personnel identification at distances up to 10 meters.

Nedap long-range readers integrate directly with ACT Pro's ANPR module for seamless license plate and vehicle access management — a popular combination for corporate parking facilities.
