World-Class Security Brands

PT Entech Security Indonesia proudly partners with global leaders to bring cutting-edge security solutions to the Indonesian market.

ACRE Security

ACRE is a global leader in providing state-of-the-art security solutions, including the ACT365, ACTpro, and CEM AC2000 access control systems. Their comprehensive portfolio offers scalable and robust security platforms for any enterprise size, from cloud-based solutions to integrated corporate security management.

Siemens SiPass

Siemens SiPass Integrated is a powerful and flexible access control system designed for high-security and large-scale applications. It provides advanced tools for managing access rights, monitoring security events, and integrating with other building management systems, ensuring comprehensive protection for critical infrastructure.

Visit Siemens SiPass Website →

DETEX

DETEX specializes in manufacturing high-quality, durable panic hardware and alarmed exit devices. Their products are essential for ensuring life safety and security in commercial, institutional, and industrial facilities, meeting stringent building codes and providing reliable emergency egress solutions.

Visit DETEX Website →

Nedap

Nedap offers innovative solutions for both access control (AEOS) and long-range identification (uPASS and TRANSIT). Their AEOS platform is renowned for its flexibility and openness, while their long-range RFID systems provide seamless and secure vehicle and personnel identification, optimizing traffic flow and enhancing perimeter security.

Visit Nedap Website →

Sielox

Sielox delivers advanced access control and security management solutions, focusing on ease of use and comprehensive system integration. Their platforms are designed to provide robust security for various applications, from small businesses to large corporations, ensuring efficient and secure operations.
