    Our Story

    Established 2012 as part of the Entech Pacific Group — Indonesia's trusted security distribution partner.

    Company Profile

    Premier Security Distributor in Indonesia

    PT Entech Security Indonesia is a premier supplier of professional security systems to system integrators and distributors across Indonesia. As a subsidiary of Entech Pacific Pte. Ltd. (Singapore), we bring world-class security brands directly to the Indonesian market.

    Founded in 2012, we started as an authorized distributor of Siemens Security Products — and continued when the Siemens security portfolio transitioned to Vanderbilt (now ACRE). Today, our team holds deep expertise across SiPass, ACT Pro, and a growing portfolio of security solutions.
