About PT Entech Security Indonesia

Our Story

Bringing world-class security solutions and expertise to Indonesia since 2012.

Company Profile

PT Entech Security Indonesia is a leading authorized distributor and solution provider for enterprise-grade security systems in Indonesia. Established in 2012, we specialize in delivering integrated access control, intrusion detection, and life safety solutions tailored to the unique needs of commercial, industrial, and institutional clients.

As part of the Entech Pacific Group, we leverage global partnerships with top-tier security brands to provide cutting-edge technology, combined with local expertise in system design, implementation, and ongoing technical support.

Our commitment is to empower organizations with intelligent and reliable security infrastructure, ensuring the safety of their people, assets, and operations.

Our Milestones

2012

Company Foundation

PT Entech Security Indonesia was founded, marking our entry into the Indonesian market as an authorized security system distributor.

Strategic Partnerships

Solution Expansion

Expanded our offerings to include advanced intrusion detection and long-range RFID systems, catering to more complex security needs.

2023
